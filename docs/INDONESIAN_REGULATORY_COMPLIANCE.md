## 1. REGULATORY FRAMEWORK OVERVIEW

### 1.1 Primary Regulatory Bodies

#### Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK) - Financial Services Authority
- **Mandate**: Primary regulator for financial services in Indonesia
- **Relevant Regulations**:
  - POJK No. 55/POJK.04/2020 concerning Investment Management Companies
  - POJK No. 13/POJK.03/2021 concerning Digital Financial Innovation
  - POJK No. 21/POJK.04/2015 concerning Investment Advisory Services

#### Indonesia Stock Exchange (IDX)
- **Mandate**: Stock exchange operations and trading rules
- **Relevant Regulations**:
  - IDX Rule Number II-A concerning Stock Listing
  - IDX Rule Number I-E concerning Trading Rules
  - IDX Rule Number I-H concerning Settlement and Clearing

#### Bank Indonesia (BI)
- **Mandate**: Payment systems and currency regulations
- **Relevant Regulations**:
  - BI Regulation No. 23/6/PBI/2021 concerning Payment Services
  - BI Regulation concerning Foreign Exchange Transactions

### 1.2 Applicable Laws and Regulations

1. **Law No. 8 of 1995** concerning Capital Market
2. **Law No. 21 of 2011** concerning Financial Services Authority
3. **Government Regulation No. 45 of 2020** concerning Financial Services Authority
4. **Law No. 27 of 2022** concerning Personal Data Protection (UU PDP)

## 2. INVESTMENT ADVISORY COMPLIANCE

### 2.1 Investment Advisory License Requirements

**Status**: ✅ **COMPLIANT - ADVISORY ONLY**

Project Aurum operates as an **investment advisory and recommendation system** and does NOT:
- Execute trades on behalf of clients
- Hold or manage client funds
- Provide discretionary investment management
- Act as a broker or dealer

### 2.2 Advisory Service Disclosures

All recommendations include mandatory disclosures:

```
INVESTMENT ADVISORY DISCLAIMER:
- This is investment advice/recommendation only
- Past performance does not guarantee future results
- All investments carry risk of loss
- Clients make independent investment decisions
- No guarantee of profitability
- Consult licensed financial advisors for personalized advice
```

### 2.3 Risk Warnings and Disclaimers

Every signal and recommendation includes:
- Risk level assessment (Low/Medium/High)
- Maximum recommended position size
- Stop-loss recommendations
- Market volatility warnings
- Indonesian market-specific risks

## 3. DATA PROTECTION AND PRIVACY COMPLIANCE

### 3.1 Indonesian Personal Data Protection Law (UU PDP) Compliance

**Status**: ✅ **FULLY COMPLIANT**

#### Data Processing Principles:
- **Lawfulness**: Processing based on legitimate business interests
- **Purpose Limitation**: Data used only for trading recommendations
- **Data Minimization**: Only collect necessary user data
- **Accuracy**: Ensure data accuracy and regular updates
- **Storage Limitation**: Data retention policies implemented
- **Security**: Comprehensive security measures implemented

#### User Rights Implementation:
- **Right to Access**: Users can view their data
- **Right to Rectification**: Users can correct their data
- **Right to Erasure**: Users can delete their accounts
- **Right to Data Portability**: Users can export their data
- **Right to Object**: Users can opt-out of processing

### 3.2 Data Localization Requirements

**Indonesian Data Residency**: ✅ **COMPLIANT**
- User personal data stored within Indonesian borders
- Data processing servers located in Indonesia
- Backup systems maintain Indonesian data residency
- Cross-border data transfer only with proper safeguards

### 3.3 Privacy Policy and Consent

**Comprehensive Privacy Policy** covering:
- Types of data collected
- Purposes of data processing
- Data sharing practices (none)
- User rights and how to exercise them
- Contact information for data protection officer
